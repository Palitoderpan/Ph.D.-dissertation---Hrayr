This folder contains the main database regarding the questionnaire "Sobrepeso y Obesidad en México" that was conducted online between 2021 & 2022.
If you want to access the full database or the R code utilized for the statistical analysis, please reach out to Hrayr Der Hagopian at hrayrdht@ciencias.unam.mx and provide a detailed explanation for your request.
DESCRIPTION.
There are 38 variables in total in the OO Survey Data.csv file:
1. ID: A unique identifier for each participant. The number was assigned according to the order in which they responded the survey.
The missing numbers are participants that were excluded because they failed to meet the inclusion criteria.
2. AGE: The participant's age in years.
3. SEX: The participant's biological sex: 1 (male) or 0 (female).
4. WEIGHT: The participant's weight in kilograms.
5. HEIGHT: The participant's height in meters.
6. BMI: The participant's Body Mass Index calculated according to their height & weight.
7. PREVENTIVE CHECK-UPS?: Whether the participant has undergone any preventive check-ups in the past six months: 0 (no) or 1 (yes).
8. DIABETES?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has diabetes: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
9. HYPERTENSION?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has hypertension: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
10. CARDIOVASCULAR?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has any cardiovascular disease: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
11. GOUT?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has gout: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
12. FATTY LIVER?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has fatty liver: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
13. COLON CANCER?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has colon cancer: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
14. SLEEP APNEA?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has sleep apnea: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
15. OSTEOARTHRITIS?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has osteoarthritis: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
16. THYROIDITIS?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has thyroiditis: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
17. WILLI-PRADER/GASTRITIS?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has Willi-Prader/Gastritis: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
18. CHRONIC FATGUE?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has chronic fatigue: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
19. BINGE EATING DISORDER?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has binge eating disorder: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
20. NIGHT EATING DISORDER?: Whether anyone in the participant's family has night eating disorder: 0 (nobody), 1 (a close relative), 2 (myself), 3 (myself & a close relative).
21: AVG MONTHLY HOUSEHOLD INCOME: The average monthly income for the participant's household considering all sources of income (e.g. scholarships).
22: % OF INCOME ON FOOD: The percentage of the participant's household income that is allocated to the purchase or preparation of food.
23: AVG DISCOUNT THETA: The estimate of the participant's parameter theta of hyperbolic discounting.
24: AVG DAYS EXERCISE: The average number of days the participant does exercise per week.
25: AVG HRS EXERCISE PER DAY: The average number of hours the participant does exercise on the days (s)he exercises.
26: AVG HRS OF SLEEP: The average number of hours the participant sleeps per night.
27: SLEEP?: The time at which the participant usually goes to sleep.
28: WAKE UP?: The time at which the participant usually wakes up.
29: # NAPS: The average number of naps the participant sleeps per day.
30: AVG NAP DURATION: The average amount of time each nap lasts for the participant.
31: TOTAL HRS SLEEP: The total amount of sleep the participant gets per day considering both sleep at night and naps.
32: # RELATIVES: The number of relatives within the participant's family.
33: # RELATIVES W/ OO: The number of relatives who are overweight or obese.
34: # FRIENDS: The number of friends the participant has.
35: # FRIENDS W/ OO: The number of friends the participant has who are overweight or obese.
36: OO PARTNER?: Whether the participant has an overweight or obese partner: 0 (no), 1 (yes), 2 (doesn't have a partner).
37: % FAMILY W/OO: The percentage of the participant's family members who are overweight or obese.
38: % FRIENDS W/ OO: The percentage of the participant's friends who are overweight or obese.
